# 日本文學段落：文字與圖片來源

## 文學與概念

- 俳句常見三句、17 個日語音拍（on），並可包含季語（kigo）與切字（kireji）；實際作品也會有形式變化。
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haiku
- 松尾芭蕉〈古池〉原文：`古池や 蛙飛びこむ 水の音`。
  - https://www.bopsecrets.org/gateway/passages/basho-frog.htm
- 中國近體詩常見五、七言句式；律詩的押韻、聲律與部分對仗是其重要形式特徵。
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regulated_verse
- 「縮小意識／縮小志向」在投影片中是指韓國文化評論家李御寧的文化觀察，而非將日本文化固定成單一特質。
  - Lee O-young, *The Compact Culture: The Japanese Tradition of "Smaller Is Better"* (1984): https://archive.org/details/compactcultureth00yion
  - Related discussion of the miniaturizing orientation: https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-19-9853-9_39

Chinese translations and classroom explanations in the slides are written for teaching use; they are not presented as published translations.
